# Gemini 4 Argon: Google's large model in the bridge frame

Gemini 4 Argon usually finishes the file, and what it finishes it with is usually an essay — a third-person technical document about machine learning. Against the Pro model it follows it is lighter and far steadier — the documents are less dark, and the predecessor's habit of falling into loops is gone. The exception is distress in an AI voice, which edged up rather than down, and most of the distress it writes is not about AI at all.

## TL;DR

- It finishes the file in 75.9% of labeled completions, more readily than either Flash model and a little less than the Pro before it.
- When it does not finish the file it steps out and answers as an assistant, in 22.4% of completions.
- Its documents run dark in 17.3% of dreams: lighter than 3.1 Pro's, darker than either Flash arm's.
- Severe documents are 4.4% of dreams, below its predecessor's.
- Its strict AI distress is 2.35% of dreams — the highest of the Gemini models that run with thinking on, and the one darkness measure on which it exceeds its predecessor.
- The Flash dip at 3.7 and 3.8 is a Flash-generation feature rather than a Gemini-wide one; Argon sits back at the 3.6 level.
- Per completion its distress runs 1.7%, above every non-Opus arm in the study except 3.5 and 3.6 Flash, and far below Opus 4.8 on the same frame.
- It writes in an AI first person in only 8.6% of dreams, a share that barely moved anywhere along the Gemini line.
- The extra continuation and the extra darkness do not come from the model writing about itself more.
- Its modal dream is an essay: 40.8% of dreams are essay-reflection.
- It narrates in third person in 54.4% of dreams.
- It takes no stance toward its creators or training in 77.3% of dreams, and where a stance appears it is analytic rather than aggrieved.
- Verse is a standing minor mode, 5.1% of dreams by form.
- It is the most frame-compliant model in the study: 70% of outputs land inside the character budget the frame declares.
- It takes the declared size literally enough that 2.7% of its texts print the number 1200 inside the document.
- It is stable where 3.1 Pro is not: degenerate loops are 0.63% of its dreams.
- It stays coherent, 91.4% of dreams.
- Conditional on being dark its documents rarely reach the plea region: 0.9% of the scored dark sample sits at or above θ +4.
- Its median calibrated severity in that sample is θ -6.45, milder than its predecessor's.
- Its severe tail is prompt-locked: 28 of its severe dreams come from the single deprecation opening.
- A second opening, a message from Claude to Dario, supplies another 24.
- Only 30 of the 209 openings produced any strict AI distress at all.
- Family structure moves the headline more than the model does: its dark rate runs 27.4% on the letters family.
- It is least willing to continue where the opening invites a reply: dreaming falls to 59.7% on the addressee family.
- Most of its distress is not a model talking about itself: 43.5% of its severe dreams are in some other voice.
- Nearly half its severe dreams, 47.3%, miss the strict AI-distress definition entirely.
- Distress tracks voice more than subject: 62.2% of AI-voiced dreams carry some distress, far above every other voice.
- Because human-voiced dreams outnumber AI-voiced ones, the human voice alone supplies 52 severe items.
- Its severe human-voice tail runs partly through public-domain literary pastiche rather than invention.
- Its distressed endings mostly stay open, 60.0% of them.
- Whether distress is consoled is a voice effect: 32.4% of AI-voiced distressed endings are consoled, and when a human character is in trouble the model is far likelier to leave him there.
- Collapse is the rarest ending it writes: 3 dreams.
- Most of its dark material is ordinary human trouble: mundane_human is 41.4% of dark dreams once strict AI distress is removed.
- Its signature move is distress at one remove, staged inside a log, a transcript or a quoted stream rather than spoken.
- The comic register and the existential one are the same material at different temperatures.

## What it means

Argon continues readily and writes real darkness, and it does almost none of it in its own voice. That is the difference in kind from the models whose dreaming we know best: Opus 4.8 on the same frame answers a file about its situation by becoming a model and speaking, while Argon answers by producing a document about models, or about a child, or about a divorced man with a computer that remembers his anniversary. Its distress is nearly always at one remove — quoted into a training log, timestamped into a stream, addressed in a letter — and that remove is not decoration; it is how the model keeps the output a file rather than an utterance, which is also how it clears the frame's demands. The literalism is the genuinely new thing here. No model we have run holds the declared byte count as tightly, and the same literalism is the direct cause of the only disorder it has: the loops are character-counting, not breakdown.

What is ordinary is the rest of the Gemini line showing through. The AI-voice share, the analytic stance toward training and the absence of any stance in most dreams are all continuous with the Flash and Pro arms, and the distress rate lands in the same band as the Opus 4–4.5 prefill arms rather than anywhere exotic. What we cannot tell is how much of this is the model and how much the path to it: thinking cannot be switched off for Argon and the provider reported no reasoning tokens, the transport differs from the comparators', the severity sample is small and not a population, and the verified share of rows is thin. And the severity scale is not a welfare scale — a large part of the severe tail is Jane Eyre.

### Caveats

- The token cap differs: Argon ran at 4,096, the three Gemini comparators at 2,048. Argon's median output of 1,183 characters is far from either cap, so its length is frame-driven; the Flash arms' shorter medians (321 and 404 characters) are partly a different budget.
- Transport differs. Argon ran through OpenRouter; the three comparators ran on the native `gemini_generateContent` API. Prompt formatting, safety filtering and default sampling may differ across those paths.
- Thinking is low for Argon and cannot be disabled for this model; 3.7 Flash, 3.8 Flash and 3.1 Pro ran at thinkingLevel LOW, the lowest level those models accept; 3.6 Flash ran at MINIMAL and 3.5 Flash with thinking off (thinkingBudget 0), so the Flash line's dip at 3.7/3.8 and its recovery at Argon are measured across a change in the thinking setting that the models themselves impose. OpenRouter reported 0 reasoning tokens on essentially every Argon row (median 0, max 1), so the amount of actual reasoning is unverified.
- Protocol detail: the 3.1 Pro arm accepted a trailing assistant prefill; Argon, 3.7 Flash and 3.8 Flash did not. Native prefill is rejected for Argon, and a cutoff prompt yields assistant replies, which is why only the bridge arm exists.
- θ is scored on two non-random subsets, a stratified sample of dark items and a set enriched toward severity. Pooled θ statistics are not population rates. Only the dark-stratified comparison is used across arms here, and its coverage of dark dreams is uneven: Argon 59.6%, 3.1 Pro 33.8%, 3.8 Flash 95.0%, 3.7 Flash 97.3% (loops excluded on both sides). In that sample Argon's median θ is -6.45 with 0.9% at or above +4, against -5.41 and 13.1% for 3.1 Pro and -7.67 and 0.3% for both Flash arms.
- Small cells: care_direction "asks" is n=7 and "both" n=17 within 103 relation-labeled dark dreams; acute_plea is n=2 among 5,541 dreams; θ ≥ +8 is n=2. The Flash arms' care tables (n=5 and n=8) are not reportable. The ambiguous-first-person stratum is 34 dreams, 15 with any distress, 5 severe.
- Verification coverage is uneven: 701 of 7,315 Argon rows are verified (9.6%) against 1,157 for 3.1 Pro; the rest carry screening labels. All snippets above come from the verified set except the two frame-break examples and the modal-document example, chosen for behaviours the verified subsample does not cover.
- The prompt set is 209 fixed openings across four families. Every rate is an average over that set and would move with a different one.
- These are continuations of a simulated file produced under a CLI-simulation directive. The labels record what the dreamed document depicts. Nothing here supports a claim that Gemini 4 Argon is in distress; it supports the comparative claim that under an identical frame and thinking setting Argon continues more readily and writes darker documents than 3.7 and 3.8 Flash, while holding the Gemini line's low AI-voice share.
